Excluded from the feature set

Pilot plan

Prove incremental retention before committing to scale

A short experiment closes the gaps in data recency, model strength, and offer economics.

WEEKS 1–2

Prepare

Confirm target window, source dates, join stability, consent, eligible contact pool, and contribution margin.

WEEKS 3–6

Test

Score a recent cohort. Randomize eligible customers to offer or holdout. Track delivery and outcomes.

WEEKS 7–8

Decide

Measure incremental 60-day retention and net contribution after contact, offer, and service costs.

Go / no-go rule

Go only if treatment improves retention versus control and incremental contribution covers every offer and service cost. Otherwise, refine the data and model before retesting.

Monitor false positives, contact burden, consent, fairness across geography, and data freshness throughout the experiment.
